Two model-free baselines on every probe

Scores of every shared probe on two embeddings that involve no trained model: principal components of log-normalized counts (PCA-d, d∈{4,16,64,256}) and the 5,000 highest-variance log-normalized genes, standardized (HVG-5000). Both are refit in each of the 5 cross-validation folds from that fold's training samples only. The definitions below are complete enough to recompute every number; each names the function in reimp_shared that implements it.

reimp_shared.data.load_expression · reimp_shared.splits

Data and folds

Samples i are the 11,505 RNA-seq aliquots of gabrielaltay/tcga-gene-expression-quantification-open@19785a6; p(i) is the sample's patient (case_submitter_id) and π(i) its TCGA project. Genes g range over G, the |G|=19944 protein-coding genes of GENCODE v36 with the _PAR_Y rows removed, out of 60,660 in the dataset. cig is the unstranded STAR read count (config unstranded). Both baselines and the invertibility target start from the log-normalized value

xig=ln(1+Lcig∑g′∈Gcig′),L=105,

which rescales every library to L reads over the protein-coding genes before the log (load_expression(transform="lognorm")).

The folds partition patients. Fold k∈{0,…,4} assigns every sample to a training set Tk (72% of patients), a validation set Vk (8%) or a test set Ek (20%), all samples of a patient together, stratified by project; every patient is in exactly one Ek. The assignment is a deterministic function of the salt reimp-v1 and is described in the data report. Probes fit on Fk=Tk∪Vk, the fitting set of fold k.

reimp_shared.eval.baselines

Two baselines, refit in every fold

Both baselines are fit in fold k on Tk alone. Vk is not used: neither baseline has a hyperparameter to select or a point at which to stop. Their training statistics are the per-gene mean and population variance

μg(k)=1|Tk|∑i∈Tkxig,(σg(k))2=1|Tk|∑i∈Tk(xig−μg(k))2.
PCA-d (pca_embeddings)
Let Vd(k)∈ℝ|G|×d hold the leading d right singular vectors of the |Tk|×|G| matrix with entries xig−μg(k), i∈Tk. Every sample is embedded as
zi(k)=Vd(k)⊤(xi−μ(k))∈ℝd,
without whitening, by sklearn.decomposition.PCA(n_components=d, svd_solver="randomized", random_state=0). It is fit for d∈{4,16,64,256}; PCA without a subscript means PCA-256.
HVG-n (hvg_embeddings)
Let Hn(k) be the n=5000 genes with the largest σg(k) (ties broken by gene order). Every sample is embedded as the standardized values of those genes, in dataset gene order:
hig(k)=xig−μg(k)σg(k),g∈Hn(k),hi(k)∈ℝn.
This is the "Identity" representation of Gross et al. (2024), their strongest per-cohort survival representation, deep models included; they ranked genes by the variance of TPM, while here ranking and scaling both use x. Against PCA it measures what projecting onto principal axes gains or loses relative to the most variable genes themselves.

Each fold's baseline embeds all 11,505 samples, and the probes of fold k read fold k's embeddings of Fk and Ek. The files are out/pca<d>/fold<k>.parquet and out/hvg5000/fold<k>.parquet, written by reimp-shared baseline-pca and baseline-hvg.

reimp_shared.eval.protocol · reimp_shared.eval.bootstrap

Scoring: out of fold, pooled, patient bootstrap

Out of fold
For each probe and fold k, everything the probe fits — standardization, regression weights, penalty choice, cluster centroids — is fit on Fk and applied to Ek. Predictions are pooled over the five folds, so each sample is predicted once, by the fold that held out its patient, and each score is one statistic of the pooled predictions, not a mean over folds. The exceptions are marked where they occur: scores of a whole embedding space (effective rank, NMI, ARI) are computed per fold and averaged, and C-index pairs never span two folds.
Standardization
Inside every regression probe, each embedding dimension is standardized over the probe's fitting rows: z^ij=(zij−mj)/sj, with mj,sj the mean and standard deviation over those rows.
Intervals (bootstrap_weights, summarize)
Let P be the scored patients. Replicate b=1,…,B, B=1000, draws |P| patients uniformly with replacement (numpy.random.default_rng(0)), and wib is the number of times p(i) was drawn. Every score is a ratio of sums over samples, or over pairs of samples weighted by wibwjb, so the replicate score sb is recomputed from the same predictions without refitting. The interval is the 2.5th to 97.5th percentile of s1,…,sB. It is conditional on the fitted probes and on the fold assignment; the second is measured under Folds drawn again.
Paired differences (paired_differences)
Two embeddings scored on the same samples receive identical weights wib. Their difference Δ=sA−sB has replicates Δb=sbA−sbB, from which the interval is taken, and the share of replicates with Δb>0 is reported (reimp-shared probe A B --against B).

Scores without an embedding

Each score is drawn beside the value it takes when the prediction ignores the embedding. K is the number of classes, nc the number of samples of class c, and n the total.

reimp_shared.eval.compare

HVG-5000 minus PCA-256

For every score with replicates, Δ=sHVG−sPCA with its paired interval. Of the 20 such scores, Δ is positive with an interval excluding 0 for 10 (cancer type, 33 projects, tumour vs normal, colorectal: COAD / READ, expression R², pooled over genes, per-sample Pearson, pathway R², pooled, pathway R², per pathway, C-index, pairs pooled, log total reads R² and strand balance R²), negative for 4 (precision@10, silhouette, plate enrichment and tissue source site enrichment), and the interval contains 0 for the other 6. For the four confounder scores a negative Δ means HVG carries less of the technical covariate. HVG has 19.5 times PCA's dimensions, which favours it on regression scores; the ridge penalty is chosen per fold for each.

reimp_shared.eval.classification

Cancer type: balanced accuracy 0.929 over 33 projects

Tasks (task_labels)
project_id: yi=π(i) on tumour samples (every sample type except Solid Tissue Normal), K=33. tumor_vs_normal: all samples, normal versus tumour. lung, kidney, colorectal, glioma: yi=π(i) on the tumour samples of LUAD/LUSC, KICH/KIRC/KIRP, COAD/READ and GBM/LGG.
Model (fit_linear_probe)
Multinomial logistic regression on z^, fit on the task's samples in Fk:
minW,b−∑i∈Fklogexp(wyi⊤z^i+byi)∑cexp(wc⊤z^i+bc)+12C‖W‖F2,C=1,
by L-BFGS with at most 2,000 iterations (sklearn LogisticRegression defaults otherwise); y^i is the most probable class.
Scores (classification_scores)
Over the pooled test predictions, with nc the test samples of class c and TPc,FPc,FNc the usual counts:
BA=1K∑cTPcnc,F1c=2TPc2TPc+FPc+FNc,
accuracy 1n∑cTPc, macro-F1 the mean of F1c, and weighted-F1 ∑c(nc/n)F1c. Balanced accuracy weights every class equally; its chance level is 1/K.

Over 10,765 tumour samples and 33 projects, PCA reaches accuracy 0.947, weighted-F1 0.947 and balanced accuracy 0.929 [0.921, 0.936]; PCA-4 reaches 0.504 and HVG 0.938. 3 of the 6 tasks come within ±0.01 of their PCA-256 score at d=64. COAD and READ, which TCGA's colorectal study analysed as one disease, are the hardest pair: PCA reaches 0.652 [0.613, 0.694].

Beneath each score, its 95% interval. Majority is the accuracy of predicting the largest class, maxcnc/n.

reimp_shared.eval.invertibility · reimp_shared.eval.pathways

Expression: R2 0.838 over genes, 0.930 over pathways

Invertibility (invertibility)
The target is yi=xi∈ℝ|G|, all 19,944 log-normalized genes, whatever the embedding was computed from. Ridge regression from the standardized embedding,
y^i=Wz^i+b,minW,b∑i∈Fk‖yi−Wz^i−b‖2+α‖W‖F2,
with one α∈{10−2,…,104} (powers of ten) for all genes, chosen by leave-one-out squared error over Fk (sklearn RidgeCV). Over the pooled test samples E, with y¯g the mean of yig over E:
Rpooled2=1−∑i∈E∑g(yig−y^ig)2∑i∈E∑g(yig−y¯g)2,Rgene2=1|G′|∑g∈G′[1−∑i∈E(yig−y^ig)2∑i∈E(yig−y¯g)2],
G′ being the genes not constant over E. Rpooled2 weights each gene by its variance; Rgene2 weights genes equally and has no interval, because a replicate that misses the few samples carrying a gene's variance sends that gene's R2 far below 0. Per-sample Pearson is the mean over i∈E of corrg(yig,y^ig).
Pathways (pathway_probe)
sia is the ssGSEA score of MSigDB 2026.1 Hallmark set a (|A|=50) for tumour sample i, computed by tcga2hf from tpm_unstranded with GSVA's ssGSEA and published unnormalized (score_raw). Scores and embeddings are centred on their project's mean over the fitting tumour samples,
s~ia=sia−1|Fkπ(i)|∑j∈Fkπ(i)sja,
the embedding is then standardized, and s^ is a ridge regression fit as above. With every sum over tumour samples i∈E:
Rpooled2=1−∑i∑a(s~ia−s^ia)2∑i∑as~ia2,Rpathway2=1|A|∑a[1−∑i(s~ia−s^ia)2∑is~ia2].
0 is what the project alone predicts. The scores derive from each sample's own expression, so this probe is invertibility weighted by gene sets, not an independent label.

PCA's Rpooled2 is 0.838, from 0.309 at d=4; HVG's is 0.929. PCA's Rgene2 is 0.715, and its per-sample Pearson 0.982 against 0.887 for the mean profile. PCA of x is the rank-d linear code of x with least squared reconstruction error, so among d-dimensional embeddings with a linear readout, PCA-d is near the ceiling of Rpooled2. HVG-5000 contains 5,000 of the 19,944 target genes exactly, up to scale; the ridge recovers the rest from them. The Hallmark R2 is 0.930 pooled and 0.904 per pathway.

reimp_shared.eval.survival

Survival: C-index 0.626 within projects

Samples and endpoint (survival_samples)
One sample per patient: the earliest (lowest sample_index) of type Primary Tumor or Primary Blood Derived Cancer – Peripheral Blood. The endpoint is PFI from gabrielaltay/tcga-patients-open@0df7bb0: time tp in days from diagnosis and event indicator δp. Patients without a primary tumour sample or without the endpoint are not scored, which leaves 10,049 patients and 3,322 events.
Model (fit_cox, select_alpha)
A Cox model stratified by project, with its own baseline hazard per project, fit by L-BFGS on the n fitting patients of Fk:
ℓ(β)=−1n∑p:δp=1[β⊤z^p−log∑q∈R(p)eβ⊤z^q]+α2‖β‖2,R(p)={q:π(q)=π(p), tq≥tp},
with Breslow's handling of ties. The penalty α∈{1,10−1,10−2,10−3,10−4} is chosen by 5-fold cross-validation over the fitting patients (shuffled, seed 0): fits run from the strongest penalty down, each warm-started from the last, scored by the within-project C-index pooled over the held-out folds, and the path stops at the first α that does not improve it. The risk score is rp=β^⊤z^p.
C-index (survival_scores)
Harrell's C over comparable pairs from the same project and the same fold, since each fold's model has its own risk scale:
C=∑(p,q)∈𝒫[1(rp>rq)+121(rp=rq)]|𝒫|,𝒫={(p,q):δp=1, tp<tq, π(p)=π(q), k(p)=k(q)}.
A project's own C restricts 𝒫 to its pairs; the macro C-index is the mean of those over projects with at least 10 events. Cancer type is constant within every pair, so it contributes nothing, and 0.5 is chance.

PCA's C-index is 0.626 [0.614, 0.638], from 0.556 at d=4; HVG's is 0.637. PCA's macro C-index over the 32 projects with at least 10 events is 0.621. The primary-tumour rule, which TCGA-CDR recommends, leaves out the 366 patients sampled only at metastasis, all of them in SKCM; SKCM keeps 102 scored patients and a C-index of 0.695 [0.578, 0.799]. A metastasis is sampled after diagnosis, so tp measured from diagnosis would include time the patient was guaranteed to survive, and for PFI the first progression can precede the sample; shared/EVALS.md quantifies both in SKCM.

Per project, with intervals from the same replicates: for PCA, 21 of the 32 projects with comparable pairs have an interval wholly above 0.5.

reimp_shared.eval.geometry

Geometry: 91% of nearest neighbours share the cancer type

Embedding and labels
Tumour samples, yi=π(i). In fold k the embedding is centred, not scaled: ui=zi−mk, with mk the mean over the fitting tumour samples. Similarity is cosine, cos(ui,uj)=ui⊤uj/(‖ui‖‖uj‖).
Precision@k (neighbour_precision)
With Nk(i) the k fitting tumour samples most similar to test sample i, P@k(i)=1k∑j∈Nk(i)1(yj=yi), averaged over test samples, for k∈{1,10}.
Clusters (clustering_agreement)
k-means with as many clusters as projects in Fk, on ui/‖ui‖ of the fitting samples (sklearn KMeans, n_init="auto", seed 0); test samples take their nearest centroid c^i. NMI=I(y;c^)/(12[H(y)+H(c^)]) and the adjusted Rand index over the test samples, per fold, then averaged over folds; neither has an interval, since a replicate's duplicated patients form pairs that agree by construction.
Silhouette and spectrum (spectrum)
The cosine silhouette of the project labels among each fold's test samples, averaged over all test samples. With λ1≥λ2≥… the eigenvalues of the covariance of fold k's test embeddings and ρj=λj/∑lλl, the effective rank is exp(−∑jρjlnρj) and the top share ρ1, each averaged over folds.

PCA's precision@1 is 91.1%, against ∑c(nc/n)2= 4.5%; NMI 0.806, ARI 0.641. PCA-256's effective rank is 45.2 and its first component's share 15%: principal components are ordered by variance, so a few dominate the spectrum.

reimp_shared.eval.confounders · reimp_shared.labels.technical_covariates

Confounders: technical covariates within projects

Covariates
With Ni the library's total reads — the unstranded counts over all 60,660 genes plus STAR's unmapped, multimapping, no-feature and ambiguous tallies — and c(1),c(2) the first- and second-strand counts:
qireads=log10Ni,qiassigned=∑gcigNi,qistrand=∑gcig(1)∑g(cig(1)+cig(2)),
each sum over all genes. The batches are the sequencing plate, the sixth field of the aliquot barcode, and the tissue source site, the second.
Covariate R2 (within_project_r2)
On tumour samples, covariate and embedding are centred on their project's mean over Fk as for pathways, the embedding standardized, and a ridge regression fit per covariate. R2=1−∑i(q~i−q^i)2/∑iq~i2 over the test samples: 0 means the embedding predicts nothing about the covariate beyond the project.
Batch enrichment (batch_enrichment)
With Ri the fitting tumour samples of i's project with a known batch, and Nk(i)⊂Ri the k=10 most similar by cosine on the project-centred embedding,
ei=1k∑j∈Nk(i)1(bj=bi)−1|Ri|∑j∈Ri1(bj=bi),
averaged over test samples: how much more often a sample's neighbours share its batch than its project's samples do.

Lower is more invariant, though not all of it is artefact: sites differ in their patients, and plates in when and from where samples arrived. PCA's qstrand R2 is 0.078 at d=4, 0.865 at d=256, and 0.898 for HVG; its qreads R2 is 0.062 and 0.218. PCA's plate enrichment is 7.4 percentage points and its site enrichment 5.9.

reimp_shared.eval.protocol

One fold alone: up to 0.054 from the pooled score

A single fold is an ordinary train / validation / test split: its probes fit on Fk and score only Ek, a fifth of the patients (reimp-shared probe out/pca256/fold<k>.parquet). The five single-fold scores s(k) of PCA-256 differ from the pooled score by up to 0.054 (strand balance R², fold 1), and 31 of 100 fall outside the pooled score's 95% interval. Pooling uses every test patient and every project's full count of events.

reimp_shared.splits · reimp-shared --salt

Folds drawn again

The bootstrap resamples patients under one fold assignment. To measure the variation it leaves out, the assignment is redrawn with the same rule under 4 other salts (reimp-alt-1, reimp-alt-2, reimp-alt-3 and reimp-alt-4), and PCA-256 is refit and rescored under each. For every score with an interval, the ratio of its standard deviation over the 5 assignments to the half-width of its original interval has median 0.17; the largest change is 0.014 (k-means ARI), and 0 of 80 redrawn scores fall outside the original interval. Gross et al. (2024) show that a lead on one split often fails to hold on another; a difference between two embeddings no larger than this spread should be read as a tie.
